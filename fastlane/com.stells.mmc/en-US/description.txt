Capture Every Info Around You, Then Do Something.

An AI-powered text recognization camera app, Memo Cam enables accurately detecting and then extracting every meaningful information such as phone numbers, addresses, dates or URLs in your numerous photos.

Then you can make a call, open maps, navigate websites even search flights, and save them all as the contacts or raw text.

Detections/Actions

• Phone Number - Save or numbers or convert into iOS contacts.
• E-Mail Address - Send a message.
• Address - Open on map
• Date - Save as Event with Calendar.
• URL - Open with Browser.
• Flight Number - Search for flights with detected numbers.
• Plain Text - Translate Your Whole Text
• Auto Detection Bot - Choosing automatically if your photos are containing the information.
